Our Story

We Bloom With Purpose

Behind every petal is a story of passion, creativity, and love. Mystical Garden was born from a dream — to make every moment more beautiful, one bloom at a time.

How It All Started

A Garden Born From Love

Mystical Garden started as a small home-based flower shop with one simple belief — that flowers have the power to make people feel truly seen and celebrated. What began as a passion project soon blossomed into a full-service floral studio serving hundreds of clients across the Philippines.

From intimate birthdays to grand wedding venues, we pour our hearts into every arrangement. Each bouquet is handcrafted with care, using only the freshest blooms and the most creative designs tailored to your story.

Today, Mystical Garden is more than a flower shop — it's a sanctuary of creativity, warmth, and floral artistry. We believe that every occasion, big or small, deserves to be adorned with beauty.

What Drives Us

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be the most cherished floral studio in the Philippines, renowned for using the eternal beauty of flowers to turn everyday moments into remarkable memories. In our ideal world, the ideal flower would be used to commemorate every milestone, celebration, and feeling.

Our Mission

To create outstanding floral arrangements with passion, imagination, and commitment providing more than just flowers, but emotions. We are dedicated to offering same-day delivery, individualized service, and designs that resonate deeply with the individuals we serve and the spirit of every occasion.
